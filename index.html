Research

I work on robot learning for humanoid and cognitive robots. The goal is a general-purpose system for long-horizon, physically complex whole-body control, in which a humanoid interacts with objects and its environment. This covers whole-body control and loco-manipulation, physics-based motion synthesis for 3D humanoids, vision-language-action models for humanoids, world-action models, and human–object and human–scene interaction.
